How It Works

Our simple 4-step process makes it easy to report, find, and recover lost items on campus.

1

Report or Post

Lost something? Report it. Found something? Post it. Include details like location, time, and photos.

2

Search & Browse

Browse through listed items or search for your lost belongings using our smart search feature.

3

Connect

Found a match? Connect with the other party through our secure messaging system.

4

Recover

Arrange a safe pickup on campus. Verify ownership and reunite with your belongings!
